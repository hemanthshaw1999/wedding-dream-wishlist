# Privacy, terms and support drafts - NOT legal advice

Historical draft: the implemented local account/report privacy notice at `/privacy` and `current-increment.md` supersede the old shared-workspace/no-auth statements. Live terms/support still require review.

These are starting review documents, not approved launch terms or guarantees. Replace bracketed fields only after the actual operator, payment model, provider, processing locations and retention requirements are reviewed. Do not publish this template as if complete.

## Local-demo notice (implemented)

Demo - no real money. Use fictional data only. This local app stores wishlist content and temporary demo sessions on the computer running it. It has no real signup, payment collection or hosted external audience. Anyone with local access can enter the fictional owner workspace. See `/privacy`.

## Proposed live privacy notice outline - not implemented

Operator: [reviewed operator/contact]. Essential processing: owner account/session management, creating/sharing wishlists, chosen gifting workflow, support, fraud/abuse prevention and necessary financial reconciliation. Specify each actual data field, purpose, legal basis/consent mechanism, recipient, processing location and retention period before collection.

Guest identity and individual amounts stay private by default. Published wishlist names/story/date/goals are visible to the chosen audience; unlisted links can be forwarded. Optional public display requires a separate explicit choice. Marketing is separate and off by default. Do not collect phone/email unless the chosen feature needs it; explain why at collection.

List actual processors, cross-border arrangements, security controls without absolute guarantees, account/data export/deletion route, financial/legal retention exceptions, outstanding refund/dispute holds, grievance contact, children/age restrictions and incident-notification process. Remove secrets and unnecessary identifiers from logs. No UPI PINs, card details, OTPs, PAN or identity documents should enter this app/chat; any authorized KYC uses the provider-managed secure process.

## Proposed live product terms outline - not implemented

The service is for voluntary wedding gifts toward a couple's experiences/goals. It is not an investment, lending, returns, charitable-fundraising or tax-receipt product. Define the actual operator, eligibility, age restrictions and permitted use. Do not promise tax benefits or characterize every wedding-related transfer as exempt.

State the selected payment model prominently. For A, a guest declaration is not verified payment; the couple checks bank records, and the platform cannot refund money it never processed. For B, identify the approved provider, actual recipient/settlement model, fees/taxes, payment states and provider-specific refund/dispute rules only after written verification. Neither model is approved by these drafts.

Define wishlist publication/visibility, content permissions, impersonation/abuse prohibition, moderation and suspension/appeal processes, goal edits/closure/expiry/overfunding policy, confirmation references, service limitations, support response expectations and applicable law/dispute resolution with counsel. Do not invent liability exclusions or statutory waivers.

## Proposed support notice outline - not implemented

Contact: [approved support channel and operator]. Provide a private contribution reference rather than a public name, bank screenshot or identity document. Never request passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or payment secrets.

Pending/unknown outcome: do not ask a guest to pay again merely because the return page timed out. Use the applicable manual verification or authoritative provider reconciliation path. Mistaken confirmations require an audited correction; provider refunds follow actual provider rules and eligibility. Support cannot silently alter balances or recipient details.

Provide abuse/impersonation reporting, urgent security contact, pause/suspension/recovery handling, privacy requests and escalation steps. Those channels and workflows do not exist yet; no emails, SMS or WhatsApp messages have been sent.
